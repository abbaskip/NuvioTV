# Stalker portal interoperability

The Stalker/MAG request, handshake and profile conventions in ReSeq's StalkerPortalClient were adapted with reference to [IPTVnator by 4gray and contributors](https://github.com/4gray/iptvnator). ReSeq implements the Android transport, channel/guide import and playback integration locally; it does not depend on IPTVnator's servers or application.
